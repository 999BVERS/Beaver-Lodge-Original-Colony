BLOC Competition Series Season 1: The Road to the Finals

Three chapters. Three challenges. Three champions. The qualifying chapters are over — meet the finalists heading into the Season 1 showdown.

BLOC Team
· August 10, 2026 · 3 min read

Three chapters. Three challenges. Three champions.

The qualifying chapters of the BLOC Competition Series Season 1 have officially come to an end. What started as a holders-only kickoff has grown into a showcase of creativity, bringing together builders from within and beyond the BLOC community.

Now, the chapter winners have earned their places in the Season 1 Finals.

Chapter I — Welcome to BLOC

The journey began with Chapter I: Welcome to BLOC, a special holders-only kickoff created to celebrate the community members who helped build the foundation of BLOC.

Participants were challenged to create a poster representing what BLOC meant to them.

Viper (@ViperrSolGodNFT) emerged as the first Chapter Champion, earning the 0.2 SOL Chapter Prize and securing a place in the Season 1 Finals.

Chapter II — Advertise BLOC

The competition then opened to a wider community with Chapter II: Advertise BLOC.

Creators were challenged to advertise BLOC through their own creative vision, finding unique ways to communicate the identity, community, and spirit of the colony.

MisterMetaX (@MisterMetaX) claimed the Chapter II title, taking home the 0.2 SOL Chapter Prize and earning a spot in the Season 1 Finals.

Chapter III — Lights. Camera. BLOC.

The final qualifying chapter brought BLOC to the big screen with Chapter III: Lights. Camera. BLOC.

Participants were challenged to imagine BLOC as a movie and transform its story and identity into a compelling movie poster.

8 (@OCTOWEST) emerged as the Chapter III Champion, receiving the 0.2 SOL Chapter Prize and securing the final spot in the Season 1 Finals.

The Finalists

The qualifying journey is now complete.

Three champions. Three different victories. One final competition.

  • 🏆 Viper (@ViperrSolGodNFT) — Chapter I Champion
  • 🏆 MisterMetaX (@MisterMetaX) — Chapter II Champion
  • 🏆 8 (@OCTOWEST) — Chapter III Champion

Each champion has proven themselves through a different challenge. Now, they will put everything they've built throughout the competition to the ultimate test.

The Season 1 Finals

The Season 1 Finals will bring the three Chapter Champions together for the final showdown of the BLOC Competition Series.

There will be one Season Champion.

And waiting at the finish line is the ultimate reward: an exclusive customized 1-of-1 BLOC Beaver.

The qualifying chapters are over. The champions have been chosen. Now, it's time to find out who will become the BLOC Competition Series Season 1 Champion.

Three champions. One final showdown. One builder will take the crown.
