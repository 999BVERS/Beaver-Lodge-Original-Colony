Chapter II Recap: Advertise BLOC

Absolutely. Chapter II should feel like the next stage after the holders-only kickoff — now opening the competition to the wider builder community.

BLOC Team
· July 28, 2026 · 5 min read

Following the holders-exclusive kickoff of Chapter I, the Beaver Lodge Original Colony Competition Series Season 1 opened its doors wider with Chapter II: Advertise BLOC.

This time, the challenge wasn't about simply introducing BLOC — it was about selling the idea of BLOC through creativity.

With the chapter open to both BLOC holders and non-holders, creators from outside the colony were invited to step in, explore what BLOC represents, and turn that understanding into an advertisement of their own.

A New Challenge, A Bigger Stage

Chapter II challenged participants to think like creative directors.

Using BLOC's identity, story, community, and vision as inspiration, participants were tasked with creating a poster that could capture attention and communicate why someone should discover BLOC.

The challenge opened the competition to a broader group of builders while maintaining the same competitive spirit established in Chapter I.

Different creators approached the brief in completely different ways — some focused on BLOC's identity, others on its community, while others took a more unconventional approach to advertising the colony.

That diversity is exactly what made Chapter II stand out.

Chapter II Champion

After the entries were reviewed by the judging panel, MisterMetaX (@MisterMetaX) emerged as the Chapter II Champion.

MisterMetaX's entry stood out through its creative direction, visual impact, and ability to communicate the BLOC concept within the advertising theme.

The victory earned MisterMetaX the 0.2 SOL Chapter Prize and, most importantly, a place in the Season 1 Finals.

There, MisterMetaX will join the other chapter champions in the race for the title of BLOC Competition Series Season 1 Champion.

The Season Finals will determine who takes home the season's grand prize, the exclusive customized 1-of-1 BLOC Beaver.

The builders are coming together. The champions are being decided. And the race for the BLOC Competition Series Season 1 Championship continues.
