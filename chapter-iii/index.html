Chapter III Recap: Lights. Camera. BLOC.

The BLOC Competition Series Season 1 continued with Chapter III: Lights. Camera. BLOC., bringing the competition into the world of cinema.

BLOC Team
· August 10, 2026 · 5 min read

The Beaver Lodge Original Colony (BLOC) Competition Series Season 1 continued with Chapter III: Lights. Camera. BLOC., bringing the competition into the world of cinema.

Following the kickoff of Chapter I and the advertising challenge of Chapter II, participants were challenged to transform the BLOC story and identity into a movie poster.

The result was another collection of creative interpretations, unique visual styles, and imaginative takes on the BLOC universe.

A Cinematic Challenge

Chapter III invited creators to imagine BLOC as a movie.

Using the story, vision, and identity of BLOC as inspiration, participants were tasked with creating a movie poster that could capture the attention of an audience and communicate a story through a single visual.

Every creator approached the challenge differently. Some focused on the characters and lore, while others explored dramatic concepts, cinematic compositions, and completely original interpretations of the BLOC world.

Together, the entries made Chapter III another memorable chapter in the Competition Series.

Chapter III Champion

After the entries were evaluated by the judging panel, 8 (@OCTOWEST) was crowned the Chapter III Champion.

The winning entry stood out through its creative direction, visual impact, and interpretation of the cinematic theme, earning the highest overall recognition from the judges.

As Chapter III Champion, 8 received the 0.2 SOL Chapter Prize and earned the distinction of becoming one of the champions of the BLOC Competition Series Season 1.

Congratulations to 8 (@OCTOWEST) on the well-deserved victory.

Spotlight on Our Participants

While one creator took the chapter title, Chapter III was built by everyone who accepted the challenge.

Each participant brought a different perspective to the theme, contributing another piece to the growing creative history of BLOC.

Another Chapter in the Books

Chapter III has officially become part of the BLOC Competition Series Season 1 story.

From Welcome to BLOC, to Advertise BLOC, and now Lights. Camera. BLOC., each chapter has brought a different challenge and a new collection of creators into the spotlight.

Congratulations once again to 8 (@OCTOWEST), and thank you to every builder who participated in Chapter III.

The competition continues to grow, one season, one chapter at a time.
